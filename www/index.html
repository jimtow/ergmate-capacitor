ErgMate

Universal metrics for any erg. Report Watt-hrs assumes 100 UserWatts and 4 ft handle pull
ErgMate provides a baseline meters per stroke based on yourcadence(SC) and split (seconds/500m).
ErgMate uses a 500m split time, and a sustainable cadence to calculate your distance per stroke (DPS) for a workout at any cadence and duration. It will soon include a timer for tracking cadence and workout duration, plus a virtual "cox" to help keep you on-cadence.
Quick steps to "calibration": Strap in and row a few strokes to estimate the cadence you can maintain for a 3-4 minute row on this erg and settings. Enter the cadence and your target 500m split (second/500m)

You're now good to go — any cadence, any distance, on this rowing machine and settings.
Notes: ErgMate mechanical watts are adjusted using 25% human efficiency.
Notes: The free version may require a calibration row.
